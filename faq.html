Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

What information is required for a consultation?

Your name, contact details and the concern you want to discuss. Kundli analysis may also require date, time and place of birth.

Can I consult through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button or request a consultation and choose WhatsApp as your preferred method.

Is my information confidential?

Your consultation information should be treated with care and used to respond to your request.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Astrology and spiritual remedies are traditional or belief-based practices and specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Can I discuss career or financial concerns?

Yes, you can request traditional astrological guidance. For financial, legal or medical decisions, consult the appropriate qualified professional.
